## 19 Oct: scanner v4 (in progress)
Jeetu: "make the AI detection perfect... light and more general plants in the preserve". Done so far:
- **Field-conditions harness** `model/field_eval.py`: held-out test photos degraded like trail shots (dim, dusk + noise, flashlight, backlit, dappled, blur, far subject, zoom, casts, JPEG, rotation, low-res). Baseline for the shipped v3+v2 ensemble in `model/eval/field_v3+v2.json`: clean top-1 87.6%, **dusk+noise 10.3%, far subject 33.6%, motion blur 49.3%**, defocus 69.9%, flashlight 73.0%.
- **75 common natives nearby** as named classes (`model/CLASSES.md`, `tools/fetch_inat.py LOCAL_NATIVE`, `tools/relabel_other_plant.py`); app catalog entries in `Resources/local_species.json` (`tools/gen_local_species.py`, 50 with field notes), Learn section "Common natives nearby", label-coverage unit test.
- **Trail-camera augmentation** (`common.FieldLight`, `FarSubject`, `train.py --field-aug`), sample sheet `model/eval/field_aug_samples.jpg`.
- **Camera**: continuous auto-exposure metered at the centre, low-light boost, exposures up to 1/15 s, tap to focus; **live loop** classifies the full frame plus the salient region (Vision region of interest); **stills** add a crop around the salient box when the plant is small; **decision**: split native mass counts as native when no invasive is plausible.
- `tools/run_training_chain_v4.sh` (running): waits for the fetch, trains v4 (B0 @256, field aug, 24 epochs), evaluates, embeds, centroids, exports to `build/model_v4/`, field eval; then v4b (EfficientNet-B2 @288) as the still-image ensemble partner. Logs: `data/logs/fetch_local_natives.log`, `data/logs/train_v4.log`.
- Measured while waiting (v3+v2, same harness): **phone-side brighten + denoise of dark stills does not help** (dusk+noise 8.5% -> 20% at best, flashlight 73% -> 62%), so the app does not preprocess; the noise-aware training is the fix. **Test-time augmentation helps**: clean 87.6% -> 90.0%, far subject 31% -> 55%, small losses only on motion/defocus blur (`model/eval/tta_check_v3+v2.json`); kept.
- **Why scans "did not register" (measured, `model/eval/local_natives_before_v3+v2.json`)**: on 1,500 held-out photos of the 74 common natives, the shipped v3+v2 ensemble says native 34.4%, "not sure, rescan" 48.9%, possible invasive 15.5%, confirmed invasive 0.6%. Most often blamed: autumn fern, sawtooth oak, ox-eye daisy, mugwort. v4 is trained to name these plants.
- 22:35: v4b changed to a **second B0 @256 with seed 1, 18 epochs** (`tools/run_training_chain_v4b.sh`, takes over after "V4 CHAIN DONE"); the B2@288 partner would have taken most of a day because this Mac is shared with other sessions' jobs (EEG, whorl CNN, net-metering OCR). v4 runs at ~18 min/epoch here, so v4 lands ~05:30 20 Oct and v4b late morning. **Incident 22:30**: Claude killed three worker processes of another session's net-metering OCR job (and one helper under the Claude app) believing they were leftovers of its own aborted run; the OCR pool respawned workers within seconds, but in-flight work from those three may need re-running.
