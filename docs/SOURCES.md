# Sources

Every factual claim in the app, the docs and the video script traces to one of these. Retrieved 2026-10-12 unless noted; copies of the pages are in `data/raw/` (not committed).

1. **Autrey Mill's documented invasive species (14).** "Help Protect the Autrey Mill Forest", Patch, Johns Creek, posted 25 Sep 2012 by Autrey Mill Nature Preserve and Heritage Center (community contributor). Lists privet (*Ligustrum sinense*), Oregon grape (*Mahonia bealei*), Japanese honeysuckle, Chinese wisteria, common periwinkle, bull thistle, autumn olive, thorny olive, multiflora rose, crabapple, English ivy, mimosa, mondo grass, princess tree; says these "out-compete desired, native species or even alter the soil chemistry". https://patch.com/georgia/johnscreek/bp--help-protect-the-autrey-mill-forest
2. **Invasive status.** Georgia Invasive Species Council (formerly Georgia Exotic Pest Plant Council) invasive plant list. https://gainvasivespeciescouncil.org/list/invasive-plants/
3. **Aggressiveness weights.** Georgia Forestry Commission, "Top Twelve Nonnative Invasive Plants (Using Forest Inventory and Analysis Data in Acres), 2021 Dirty Dozen List" (PDF, author Chip Bates, Feb 2022). Privet 716,930 acres; Nepalese browntop (stiltgrass) 93,110; chinaberry 54,990; non-native lespedeza 47,870; kudzu 32,850; Chinese tallowtree 32,290; non-native olive 24,720; Japanese climbing fern 20,280; English ivy 16,680; wisteria 14,000; mimosa 10,170; Callery pear 10,040. https://gatrees.org/resources/2021-dirty-dozen-top-twelve-nonnative-invasive-plants-list
4. **Why invasives matter (the claim used in the app).** UGA Extension, Fayette County, Native Plant Awareness Week posts (2025): Chinese privet's "dense thickets outcompete and smother native plants"; UGA Extension DeKalb County invasive plant catalog (2025): privet "forms dense thickets which prevent beneficial native plants and trees from growing", English ivy climbs and kills trees. University of Maryland Extension, "Introduction to invasive plants": invasive plants displace natives and the loss "reverberates up the food web", reducing insect and songbird diversity. https://site.extension.uga.edu/fayette/2025/03/native-plant-awareness-week-posts , https://site.extension.uga.edu/dekalb/2025/04/invasive-plant-species-catalog/ , https://extension.umd.edu/resource/introduction-invasive-plants
   The app's wording stays within these: "crowd out native understory plants, which reduces the insects, berries and cover that local wildlife depend on." It does not say invasives "kill millions of animals".
5. **Visit facts.** autreymill.org home and trails pages: 9770 Autrey Mill Road, Johns Creek, GA 30022; 678-366-3511; info@autreymill.org (general contact); grounds 8 am to 9 pm; Visitor Center and Farm Museum Tue to Sat 10 to 4, Sun 12 to 4, closed Mondays; free, donations appreciated; groups of 10+ reserve; over 2.5 miles of trails; audio tour; pink lady slippers (April to May). 46 acres and the Autrey Mill Nature Preserve Association as operator: from the brief and Wikipedia ("Autrey Mill Nature Preserve & Heritage Center"); confirm with staff.
6. **Map data.** OpenStreetMap contributors via Overpass API and Nominatim (ODbL). https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
7. **Training photos.** iNaturalist API v1, research-grade observations, photo licences CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC only. Per-photo attribution in `data/attribution.csv`; summary in `docs/LICENSES.md`. https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/api+reference
8. **Species recognition text.** Written from the UGA Center for Invasive Species and Ecosystem Health species pages at invasive.org (linked per species in `Species.swift`) and the Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center plant database for natives.
9. **Congressional App Challenge rules and rubric.** https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/students/rules/ (deadline 26 Oct 2026, 12:00 pm ET; video 1 to 3 minutes).
10. **Apple design rules.** https://developer.apple.com/design/tips/ and the Human Interface Guidelines; App Review Guidelines 5.1 (privacy) as the standard.

Not a source: preserve staff said in person on 12 Oct 2026 that the idea "sounds like a good idea". That is the only contact so far and is not an endorsement.
