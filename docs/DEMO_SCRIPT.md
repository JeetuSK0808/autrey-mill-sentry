# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Format: phone screen recording (Settings > Control Center > Screen Recording) plus a few on-site clips shot on a second phone or by a friend. Voice-over recorded separately and laid over. Keep every claim to what the files show; numbers in [brackets] come from `model/eval/compare_v4b_v4.json`, `model/eval/field_v4+v4b.json`, `Resources/model_card.json` and the field test CSV or they are cut.
